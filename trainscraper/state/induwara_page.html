How it works

Line status, checked 2026-10-17: the times on this page are the schedule Sri Lanka Railways publishes and will resume to, not a promise that every section is open today. Peradeniya Junction to Kandy and Gampola to Nanu Oya are closed for Cyclone Ditwah repairs, so there is no through Colombo Fort to Badulla train at the moment and no train from Colombo is reaching Kandy.

Reopened so far: Colombo Fort to Gampola on 2 October 2026 and Nanu Oya to Badulla on 20 June 2026. Shuttles also run inside the closed gap, between Nawalapitiya and Hatton, but they cannot be reached from Colombo. SLR engineers have targeted December 2026 for the Nawalapitiya to Badulla stretch, which is a stated plan and not a confirmed reopening date. Check railway.gov.lk or hotline 1971 before you travel.

This finder reads a typed dataset of scheduled Sri Lanka Railways services, one record per train, holding its number, name, type, the classes it sells, its days of operation and its ordered list of stops with arrival and departure times copied from the SLR timetable. When you choose two stations, a pure query runs entirely in your browser:

  1. Match serving trains. A service qualifies only if its stop list contains a stop at your origin with a departure time, followed later by a stop at your destination with an arrival time. Direction matters, the return service is a separate record, so a Kandy → Colombo search never returns the Colombo → Kandy train.
  2. Read the times. Departure is the origin stop's time; arrival is the destination stop's time.
  3. Compute the duration. The journey length is the minutes between the two times. If the arrival clock reads earlier than the departure, the train has crossed midnight, so the tool adds 24 hours (1,440 minutes) and marks the arrival with a +1 day badge. This is what happens on the night mails.
  4. Apply your filters. The day-of-travel filter drops services that don't run on the selected day (weekday-only office trains disappear on a Sunday); the class filter keeps only trains that sell a class you picked.
  5. Sort by departure. Results are ordered earliest first, and a client-side clock in Asia/Colombo time highlights the next upcoming departure.

Every scheduled time is transcribed verbatim from the Sri Lanka Railways timetable. As an integrity check, the dataset carries a cross-check that re-derives each journey's duration by walking the intermediate stops one hop at a time, arrival, dwell, next departure, and confirms it equals the plain end-to-end difference. That catches a mistyped time that a simple subtraction would hide. The station list is shared with the Sri Lanka Train Fare Calculator, so the two tools always agree on station names, and each result row deep-links there with your route pre-filled to price the ticket.

Worked examples

Colombo Fort → Kandy (any day)

  1. Match: the dedicated Colombo–Kandy intercity trains (1077, 1001, 1045) all stop at Fort with a departure, then at Kandy with an arrival.
  2. 1001 Kandy Intercity: dep 07:00, arr 09:34.
  3. Duration: 09:34 − 07:00 = 2h 34m. Arrival ≥ departure, so no +1 day.
  4. Result: three trains listed, sorted by departure, 05:30 (1077, weekdays), 07:00 (1001), 15:35 (1045).

Colombo Fort → Badulla on the Night Mail (overnight)

  1. 1035 Badulla Night Mail: dep 20:00, arr 05:53.
  2. 05:53 (= 353 min) is earlier on the clock than 20:00 (= 1,200 min), so the train has crossed midnight.
  3. Duration: (353 + 1,440) − 1,200 = 593 minutes = 9h 53m.
  4. The arrival shows a +1 day badge. You board tonight and arrive tomorrow morning.

Day filter: Colombo Fort → Kandy on a Sunday

  1. Set the day of travel to “Sunday”.
  2. Train 1077 is a weekday-only office intercity, so it is dropped.
  3. Trains 1001 and 1045 run daily, so they remain and the 05:30 departure disappears from the list.
  4. This is how the tool hides services that don't run on your chosen day.

Frequently asked questions

Pick Colombo Fort as your from-station and Kandy as your to-station and the finder lists every direct Kandy intercity train sorted by departure, with a live countdown to the next one. The dedicated Colombo–Kandy intercity services leave Fort around 05:30 (weekdays), 07:00 and 15:35 and reach Kandy in about 2 hours 35 minutes. Important as at 2026-10-17: trains from Colombo are not reaching Kandy. The Peradeniya Junction to Kandy section is closed while the Peradeniya Black Bridge is rebuilt after Cyclone Ditwah, and services from Colombo currently terminate at Peradeniya or Gampola. The times above are the published schedule SLR resumes to, so confirm on railway.gov.lk or hotline 1971 before travelling.

On the published schedule the up-country expresses (Podi Menike and Udarata Menike) run Colombo Fort to Badulla in roughly 9 hours, calling at Peradeniya, Nanu Oya, Haputale, Bandarawela and Ella, and the overnight Badulla Night Mail leaves Fort at 20:00 and reaches Badulla at 05:53 the next morning, about 9 hours 53 minutes. As at 2026-10-17 that journey cannot be made end to end: the Gampola to Nanu Oya section is still closed for Cyclone Ditwah repairs. Nanu Oya to Badulla, the Ella leg, reopened on 20 June 2026 but is cut off from Colombo, so the finder computes the scheduled duration while this notice tells you what is running.

The Jaffna Night Mail departs Colombo Fort at about 20:30 and arrives in Jaffna around 04:45 the following morning, calling at Kurunegala, Maho, Anuradhapura, Vavuniya and Kilinochchi. Because it crosses midnight, the finder shows a “+1 day” badge on the arrival time so you know it lands the next day.

The Ruhunu Kumari intercity between Colombo Fort and Matara (via Galle) offers 1st, 2nd and 3rd class plus an Observation Saloon. Set the class filter to “1st” for Colombo Fort → Galle and only the services selling first class remain. The Sagarika and Galu Kumari expresses run 2nd and 3rd class only.

Most named long-distance services, Udarata Menike, Podi Menike, Yal Devi, Ruhunu Kumari and the night mails, run daily, including Sundays and Poya (full-moon) days. Weekday-only office trains, such as the early Kandy and Chilaw office intercities, do not run at weekends. Use the “Day of travel” filter to hide services that don’t run on your day.
